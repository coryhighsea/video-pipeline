## LinkedIn / long post

Registering a company in Canada: pay a fee online. Done.

Registering a company in Germany: 👇

1. Pick your legal form (we went UG for liability protection)
2. Fill out the Musterprotokoll
3. Book an online notary (15-minute meeting, in German)
4. Open a business bank account and deposit the starting capital
5. Wait ~4 weeks for the Handelsregister
6. Pay €573 in notary and court fees
7. Transparenzregister
8. Gewerbeamt (€33)
9. Finanzamt questionnaire, then more documents, then more waiting
10. Finally: a tax number and your first invoice

About four months, start to finish. And that's before the big question: Kleinunternehmer (VAT exempt) or not?

I wrote it all down, every step, cost and mistake to avoid, as the checklist I wish I'd had when we started.
